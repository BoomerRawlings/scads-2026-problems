# Inference research and experiment design

Research checked 2026-10-06. Planning only; no models trained or reported results independently reproduced. Sources below are original papers, publisher pages, or author repositories. Recommendations are our proposed design, not findings from completed experiments.

## Essential distinction

The primary target should be **an employee's direct manager during a stated interval**, with an explicit unknown outcome. Maintain separate relations for ancestor/superior, team membership, communication, situational authority, and influence. High centrality, authoritative language, or seniority does not identify a direct manager. Unknown ground truth is not a negative label.

Three experiments must remain separate: (1) complete a partially known hierarchy; (2) infer a hierarchy from communications with no hierarchy supplied as input; (3) transfer the learned scorer to a different organization. Success on (1) does not establish (2) or (3).

Evaluation: 43 Enron employees, relationships with at least five emails each direction, leave-one-ego-network-out validation. Best reported content ranker MRR 0.719; traffic ranker 0.518. | Closest classical baseline to our analyst workflow. Reimplement traffic, content, and combined rankers. Keep centrality/rank baselines but do not mistake their output for supervision edges. |
| [Bramsen et al., ACL 2011](https://aclanthology.org/P11-1078.pdf), *Extracting Social Power Relationships from Natural Language* | Word/POS patterns and SVMs distinguish upward/downward communication. | Reproducible methodological baseline: lexical classification. It measures relative social power; it does not recover which superior is the immediate manager. |

No peer/unrelated class; no employee-disjoint or company-transfer guarantee. Random thread splits can retain the same people and dyads across partitions; audit this explicitly. |

Greetings/signatures masked; this does not prove all identity leakage removed. Paper identifies expertise and politeness as confounds. Author model code not verified; architectural replication possible. |

No verified author code located. |
| [Zang et al., ESWA 270, 25 April 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417424032159), *Power on graph* / PRM-GNN | GNN explicitly targets manager–subordinate and advisor–advisee relations. Publisher reports Enron and Coauthor experiments. | Relevant modern GNN comparator, beyond role classification. Public abstract/preview verified; full label provenance, splits, negative sampling, code, and exact reproduction remain unresolved. Do not import headline improvements into our expected accuracy. |

## Data and validity consequences

The [Agarwal gold-standard paper](https://aclanthology.org/P12-2032.pdf) describes 1,518 employees and 13,724 **dominance pairs**, including non-immediate ancestry. These must not all become direct-manager labels. Employee correspondence coverage differs substantially from complete-mailbox coverage. The [Columbia schema](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~rambow/enron/Enron.html) also documents that alias consolidation sometimes merged an executive and assistant. Entity-resolution mistakes can therefore masquerade as hierarchy errors.

Before training, freeze an auditable relation map: original label, directness, employee/unit type, source chart, interval, ambiguity, and adjudication. Preserve multiple concurrent roles and changed managers. A transitive reduction is meaningful only for a correctly typed, time-consistent, sufficiently observed graph; missing intermediate people can turn apparent adjacency into a false direct report.

Our proposed evaluation protections:

- Deduplicate quoted/forwarded content across splits; group complete threads and repeated dyads. Fit vocabulary, normalizers, candidate thresholds, and calibrators on allowed partitions only.
- Run historical replication splits separately from temporal, employee-disjoint, and department-held-out splits. Ensure graph message passing never receives held-out hierarchy labels, inverses, or derived ancestor features.
- Compare raw names/signatures against pseudonymized content and explicit-title masking. A pretrained LLM might already know Enron names or charts; masking is a stress test, not proof of no contamination.
- Evaluate dense-mailbox and sparse-correspondent strata separately. Simulate mailbox/edge thinning; missing traffic is different from weak evidence of authority.
- Use verified non-manager candidates where possible. Do not train every unlabeled pair as negative. Report candidate-generation recall before scoring accuracy.

## Recommended baseline sequence

1. **Sparse candidates and transparent rankers.** Compare top communication neighbors, shared-neighbor candidates, and retrieved explicit reporting mentions. Include an unknown/out-of-corpus-manager option. Start with degree/volume rules, logistic regression, and a small boosted-tree or pairwise linear ranker. Features: directional volume, reciprocity, To/CC, broadcast fraction, thread initiation, actual reply timing, common contacts, time overlap, and missingness indicators. Never interpret a rank score as probability without calibration.
2. **Lexical and authority features.** Reproduce word/POS SVM direction classification; add thread features. Train a direct-manager ranker separately. Use modern text encoders and an LLM to extract bounded evidence claims: assignment, approval, status report, explicit reporting statement, deference, and plausible alternative explanation. Require message IDs/spans, temporal scope, and unknown; aggregate through the ranker.
3. **Graph comparisons.** Reproduce HyperTransE completion where inputs can be rebuilt. Compare a shared-parameter inductive GraphSAGE/GAT edge scorer using the same candidate set and evidence features; no employee-ID embeddings for transfer. Attempt PRM-GNN only after its full evaluation protocol is available. Graph density and pair-node construction cost must be measured.
4. **Optional structure projection.** Compare independent choices with cycle-free forest decoding and an unknown root. Preserve alternatives and raw scores. Human-approved constraints override proposals but remain separately recorded. Audit calibration after candidate pruning, top-one selection, and structural projection.

Required ablations: metadata/text/combined; identity masked/unmasked; one-hop/two-hop/retrieval candidates; direct-report versus ancestor supervision; random versus hard negatives; graph messages disabled; temporal windows; unknown enabled/forced parent; decoder on/off; LLM evidence extraction versus direct prompting; single versus multi-agent prompting under equal cost.

Primary metrics: candidate recall, direct-parent top-1 and MRR, edge precision/recall on verified labels, precision–coverage under abstention, calibration on the displayed edge population, and analyst evidence-verification time. Secondary metrics: dominance direction, team membership, cycles, depth/span distortion. Structural validity alone never proves correctness. For an unlabeled transfer corpus, report coverage, stability, shift diagnostics and blinded sampled adjudication; no fabricated accuracy or calibration claim.

## Defensible contribution

Candidate contribution: evidence-grounded direct-manager reconstruction that remains useful under sparse observation and organizational transfer, measures post-selection uncertainty, and records analyst corrections without overwriting provenance. Neither LLM power detection, graph-based hierarchy inference, nor human-guided evidence review is new in isolation. This targeted search includes 2026 organizational LLM systems; it is not a novelty guarantee. Final novelty review should trace citations and compare against PRM-GNN and Corporate Digital Twins once their full evaluation details are obtained.
